# Tester Recruitment Plan

Goal: recruit at least 15 real Android testers so 12 remain opted in for the required 14 continuous days.

Google Play requires at least 12 testers opted in to closed testing for 14 continuous days before production access for newer personal developer accounts. Use 15 testers as the working target so one or two people dropping off does not reset the plan.

## Best Tester Profiles

Prioritize people who can test the app naturally:

- Android users who watch Japanese YouTube, anime, VTubers, streamers, or lectures.
- Japanese learners who want English captions.
- People with different Android brands: Samsung, Pixel, OnePlus, Motorola, etc.
- At least 2 people willing to test browser playback, not just the YouTube app.
- At least 2 people willing to test live chat or portrait/landscape rotation.

## Recruitment Sources

- Friends and family with Android phones.
- Discord servers for anime/Japanese learning.
- Reddit communities, if their rules allow beta requests.
- Coworkers or classmates.
- Existing followers/social channels.
- Local language-learning groups.

## Tester Requirements

Each tester needs:

- Android 10 or newer.
- A Google account email for Play closed testing.
- Willingness to remain opted in for 14 continuous days.
- Willingness to install the app once and report whether it works.

## What Testers Should Do

Ask each tester to complete at least one pass:

1. Install from the Play testing link.
2. Open Setup & Settings.
3. Grant microphone and overlay permission.
4. Keep language as Japanese -> English (US).
5. Run Test Internal Audio Capture while a Japanese video is playing.
6. Try Start Fast Internal Captions.
7. Try mic fallback if internal audio is silent.
8. Open Capture Troubleshooting and read the result.
9. Send feedback using the feedback template.

## Daily Tracking

Track these dates carefully:

- Date tester was added to closed test.
- Date tester accepted/opted in.
- Date 14-day clock completes.
- Whether they stayed opted in.
- Whether they gave feedback.

Do not apply for production access until at least 12 testers are still opted in and have been opted in for 14 continuous days.

## Buffer Rule

Recruit 15 to 20 people. Assume some people will:

- Forget to opt in.
- Use iPhone instead of Android.
- Opt out accidentally.
- Never test.
- Not respond.
